# Academic Council Scorecard Rubric & Chairman Template

Scoring criteria and mathematical reconciliation rules for the Academic Council evaluation.

---

## 1. Scoring Dimensions

Each proposal is scored on a 1.0 to 5.0 scale across these dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight | What It Measures |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Feasibility** | 25% | Can the data actually be collected? Are variables measurable from public BEI sources? How long will data collection take? |
| **Novelty (Kebaruan)** | 20% | Does the proposal address a genuine research gap? Is it distinct from prior studies? Does it use a current phenomenon? |
| **Methodological Rigor** | 20% | Is the statistical method appropriate? Are proxies correctly formulated? Is sampling criteria defensible? |
| **Theoretical Coherence** | 15% | Does the theory logically produce the hypotheses? Is the causal mechanism articulated, not just name-dropped? |
| **Defense Readiness** | 10% | Can the student defend this proposal against tough examiner questions right now? |
| **Citation Integrity** | 10% | Are supporting references real, verifiable, and from reputable sources? |

**Composite Score** = Weighted average across all dimensions.

---

## 2. Score Anchors

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **5.0** | Exceptional | Publication-ready quality. Could be submitted to a SINTA 1 / Scopus Q2 journal with minor revisions. Requires concrete proof to award. |
| **4.0** | Strong | Minor gaps that are easily fixable. Ready for proposal seminar (seminar proposal) with confidence. |
| **3.0** | Adequate | Meets minimum requirements but has notable weaknesses. Needs 1-2 rounds of revision before seminar. |
| **2.0** | Weak | Fundamental issues in methodology, theory, or feasibility. Needs major restructuring. |
| **1.0** | Critical | Proposal is not viable in current form. Core concept or data source is flawed. |

---

## 3. Anti-Sycophancy Rules

> [!CAUTION]
> - Scores >= 4.50 on any dimension are **prohibited** unless backed by specific evidence (e.g., exact journal citation confirming methodology, verified BEI data availability screenshot, etc.).
> - Every council seat MUST surface at least **2 real weaknesses** per proposal. "No significant issues found" is not an acceptable finding.
> - The Defense Simulator seat must generate questions that genuinely challenge the proposal, not softball questions with obvious answers.

## 5. Arithmetic Reconciliation

The Chairman must verify:
- All dimension scores fall within [1.0, 5.0] range
- Composite = sum of (dimension_score × weight) for each proposal
- Ranking order matches composite scores (no contradictions)
- Weight percentages sum to 100%
